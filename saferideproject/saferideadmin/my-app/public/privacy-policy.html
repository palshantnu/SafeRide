This Privacy Policy applies to Sigi Ride's mobile application, sigiride.com, and all related services. By using our Platform you agree to the practices described here. Please read carefully before using our services.

Information We Collect 01
Personal Information

Name, phone number, email, and profile photo at registration. Drivers also provide vehicle details, driving license, and government ID documents.

Location Data

Real-time GPS location from both riders and drivers during trips for accurate navigation. Background location may be collected with your permission.

Transaction & Payment Data

Transaction history, wallet balance, and withdrawal records. Card details are processed by secure payment partners — never stored on our servers.

Device & Usage Data

Device identifiers, OS version, app version, IP address, and usage patterns to improve performance and experience.

How We Use Your Information 02
Service Delivery

Match riders with drivers, process payments, send booking confirmations and receipts, and provide real-time support.

Safety & Security

Verify driver identity, detect fraud, resolve disputes, and ensure the safety of all platform users.

Platform Improvement

Anonymized data helps analyze ride patterns, optimize routing, and develop new features tailored to user needs.

Communications

Ride updates, offers, safety alerts, and policy changes via SMS, email, or push notifications.

Information Sharing 03
Between Riders & Drivers

Limited info (name, photo, vehicle details, phone) is shared between rider and driver only to facilitate the trip.

Business Associates

Authorized associates may access driver performance data within their scope per their agreement with Sigi Ride.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information to comply with applicable laws or lawful government requests.

We Never Sell Your Data

Sigi Ride does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing — ever.

Data Security 04
Encryption

All transmitted data uses TLS/SSL encryption. Sensitive data at rest is protected with AES-256 encryption.

Access Controls

Data access is restricted on a need-to-know basis. All staff undergo background checks and security training.

Incident Response

In case of a breach, we notify affected users and authorities within the timeframe required by law.

Your Rights & Choices 05
Access & Correction

View and update your personal information anytime through the Sigi Ride app under Profile Settings.

Data Deletion

Request account deletion by contacting privacy@sigiride.com. Some data may be retained for legal compliance.

Opt-Out of Marketing

Update notification preferences in the app, or click 'unsubscribe' in any email to opt out at any time.

Location Permissions

Revoke location access in your device settings anytime, though it may affect some app features.

Cookies & Tracking 06
Cookies

Our website uses cookies to remember preferences and analyze traffic. Control cookies through your browser settings.

Analytics

Anonymized analytics data is used solely to improve our services and understand user interactions.

⚠️

Children's Privacy

Sigi Ride is not directed to individuals under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors. If a minor has provided data, email us at privacy@sigiride.com and we'll delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
